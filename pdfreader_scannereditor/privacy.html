Privacy Policy

PDF Reader - Scanner & Editor

This Privacy Policy explains how PDF Reader - Scanner & Editor handles information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the application, certain information may be processed to provide and improve its features.

This may include:

  • Information about PDF documents or images that you choose to open, scan, or edit.
  • Basic device information, such as device model, operating system version, and application version.
  • Technical information, such as crash reports and diagnostic data, where applicable.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting customer support.

2. Files and Permissions

PDF Reader - Scanner & Editor may request access to files, photos, or the camera when you use features that require these functions.

These permissions may be used to:

  • Open and read PDF documents.
  • Select images or documents from your device.
  • Scan documents using your camera.
  • Create, edit, and manage PDF files.

You can manage or revoke permissions through your device settings. The application accesses files and device functions as needed to provide the features you choose to use.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • To provide PDF reading, scanning, and editing functions.
  • To process documents selected by you.
  • To maintain and improve application performance.
  • To identify and resolve technical problems.
  • To respond to user feedback and support requests.
  • To protect the application and comply with applicable laws.

4. Document Storage and Processing

Documents you open, scan, or edit may be processed locally on your device or through other services required by specific features.

The storage and processing method may depend on the feature you use. Please review the app's actual functionality and avoid importing documents containing sensitive information unless you are comfortable with how they are handled.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared when necessary to provide application functions, respond to your requests, comply with legal obligations, protect users and our rights, or support business operations.

Third-party services, if used by the application, may process information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute data security.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy or to meet applicable legal requirements.

Documents saved on your device may remain there until you delete them.

8. Children's Privacy

Our application is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where parental consent is required by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

9. Your Choices

You can manage application permissions through your device settings and stop using the application at any time.

If you have questions or requests concerning information you have provided directly to us, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the application or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be made available through the application or its store listing.
